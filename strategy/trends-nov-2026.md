# Reels research, November 2026, and the v2 edit style

## What drives reach (consensus of 2026 guides; not official Instagram documentation)
- **Watch time and replays** are the top signal. **DM shares** come second, ahead of likes.
- **First 3 seconds:** up to ~50% of viewers drop off there. The hook must work in frame 0.
- **About half of viewers watch muted.** The hook has to be readable on-screen text, not only audio.
- **Shot length:** 2026 editing guides recommend 2–3 s at most. Hard cuts on the beat read as premium, and crossfades on every cut read as slow.
- **Format beats audio:** a trending audio peaks in 7–10 days, while a format lasts 3–4 weeks.
- **5 hashtags maximum** on Reels (Nov 2026).

## v2 edit rules
- Frame 0: a big bold hook (Inter Bold, 92 px) over a moving or zooming shot. No logo intro.
- 120 BPM grid (0.5 s beats). Shots of 0.75–2 s with hard cuts.
- Photos get a fast push-in or pull-out reveal; walking clips are sped up 1.3–1.6×.
- Length 8–12 s. End on a frame that loops back to the start, or on the strongest wide shot.
- Labels (times, numbers) carry the story when muted.
- Audio: a track from the Instagram library at upload. The renders carry a TEMP beat only so the cuts can be judged.
